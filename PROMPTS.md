# Image prompts — The Ascent (Snowmoon Ch. 6)
Shared art direction for ALL stills (append to every prompt):
Cinematic film still, 16:9, cold midday daylight, muted blue-grey stone and
pale sky, photorealistic, subtle film grain, quiet solemn mood, no text,
no watermark. The figure: a person in a dark hooded Veridian privacy robe
with a removable face covering — face NEVER visible — dark heavy fabric,
a thin neck band, heeled shoes; shorter than average stature.

Consistency anchors to repeat verbatim in every prompt with the figure:
"same dark hooded privacy robe, face fully covered, dark matte fabric"

- shot1_path: Wide shot from behind: a lone robed figure walks away from
  camera on a path where a quiet futuristic city street dissolves into
  forest; large stone-brick houses at the edges; cold midday light.
- shot2_turn: Interior of a stone tunnel at a Y-junction, two diverging
  passages; the robed figure stands in silhouette at the fork, turning
  toward the left passage; a sky bridge visible behind.
- shot3_staircase_wide: Extreme wide: a single straight outdoor staircase,
  impossibly tall (200 metres), cut into a steep mountainside; one tiny
  robed figure climbing; pale sky, cold daylight, vertigo scale.
- shot4_climb: Side view, closer: the robed figure climbing endless stone
  steps, slightly winded; wind moving the robe hem; close enough to see a
  wrist watch and a small hand device tucked at the robe.
- shot5_top: From dark tunnel mouth looking out: an open-air circular tower
  top, stone walls, open sky above, bright midday; the robed figure steps
  into the centre.
- shot6_circle_dark: Wide circle composition: ten robed figures seated in
  chairs around the edge of a circular stone tower-top chamber open to the
  sky, all faces covered, motionless; in the centre stands the lone figure;
  beside him a large dim green ring set into the stone floor/wall, faintly
  glowing. Wall lights OFF.
- shot7_circle_lit: SAME composition as shot6 (edit from shot6): the green
  ring now glows bright, and previously hidden wall lights around the stone
  wall are lit in a unique vivid green shade; the seated figures subtly
  tense, a few leaning forward; the central figure's watch touches the ring.
- shot8_device: Extreme close-up: a hand device held in a robed hand, its
  screen glowing green with a simple confirmation interface (abstract UI,
  minimal — final text will be added in post, keep screen mostly abstract
  shapes), stone and green light bokeh behind.
- endcard_bg (optional): the circular tower top empty at midday, green ring
  dimming, stone walls and sky — for the end card background.
