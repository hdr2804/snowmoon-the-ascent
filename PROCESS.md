# PROCESS.md — how "The Ascent" was made

## 1. Scene selection
Read all 32 chapters of Snowmoon (GPL v3). Competitor submissions to the
bounty already covered chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 29. Chapter 6 was chosen:
a single character, one location, minimal dialogue, visually distinctive
(the 200-metre staircase, the anonymous ritual, the green circle), and no
face-consistency problems since every face is hidden by privacy robes.

## 2. Scene verification
The tower sequence was re-read and verified beat by beat before storyboarding:
midday, cold air; path from Kalimar district merging into forest; sky bridge,
tunnel, Y-junction, left turn; a straight 200 m outdoor staircase on a
mountainside (~10 minute climb in the privacy robe); tunnel + two floors of
stairs to an open tower top with stone walls and open sky; ten Order members
in chairs around the edge, all in privacy robes; a green circle beside
Gladias, larger and brighter than usual; watch tap → wall lights ignite in a
unique green shade; the solemn, mostly silent exchange (they must not know
his name — cryptography verifies the profile); hand-device tap →
"Request confirmed." The chapter ends there.

Gladias's age and physical appearance are NOT stated in the novel, so the
film deliberately never shows his face — this is both faithful and a
production advantage.

## 3. Stills (AI image generation)
9 key stills at 16:9, all sharing one art-direction block: cold midday
daylight, muted blue-grey stone, pale sky, photorealistic, subtle film
grain, quiet solemn mood, no text, no watermark — plus a repeated
consistency anchor: the same dark hooded privacy robe, face fully covered,
dark matte fabric, thin neck band, heeled shoes.

Prompts per shot are in PROMPTS.md. shot7 ("lit") was generated by *editing*
shot6 ("dark") with the same composition, so the dark→lit transition at the
watch tap is a true crossfade of the same framing (bright green ring, wall
lanterns lit green, some figures leaning forward, watch touching the ring).

## 4. QC (visual check of every still)
- shot1 (path): PASS — figure from behind, face hidden, city dissolving
  into forest, stone-brick houses, cold midday.
- shot2 (turn): PASS — Y-junction tunnel, sky bridge behind, silhouette,
  face covered.
- shot3 (staircase wide): PASS — impossibly tall straight staircase on the
  mountainside, tiny figure, vertigo scale.
- shot4 (climb): PASS — face fully covered, wrist watch + hand device
  visible, wind in the robe hem, heeled shoes.
- shot5 (top): PASS — from dark tunnel mouth into the open circular tower
  top, figure stepping into the centre, face hidden.
- shot6 (circle, dark): PASS with a noted deviation — the generator drew
  ~12–13 seated figures instead of the novel's ten. Composition, robes,
  dim green ring and dark wall lights are correct; faces all hidden.
  Accepted (regenerating risked worse consistency); documented here.
- shot7 (circle, lit): PASS — same composition as shot6, bright green ring,
  green wall lanterns, watch touching the ring, faces hidden.
- shot8 (device): PASS — abstract green UI on the hand device, hood and face
  covering visible, no face. The text "Request confirmed." is added in post.
- endcard_bg: PASS — empty tower top, dim green ring, no people.

## 5. Motion (AI video generation attempt — FAILED)
One image-to-video attempt (shot3) returned HTTP 403: "Video generation
isn't available in your country or region." Per project rules the attempt
was stopped immediately — no retries. The film is therefore **stills-only**:
all motion is deterministic ffmpeg `zoompan` (Ken Burns): slow push-ins
(shots 1, 3, 6, 8), a slow pan (shot 2), a drift-up (shot 4), a reveal
zoom-out (shot 5), and a continuing push-in through the dark→lit crossfade
(shot 7). Crossfades of 1 s join all shots; shot7's internal dark→lit
crossfade (offset 3 s, duration 1.5 s) lands on the watch tap.

## 6. Audio (100% synthesized, documented)
No voiceover, no stock music. Built deterministically with numpy
(`make_audio.py`, fixed seed 7), 83 s at 48 kHz stereo:
- **Wind:** white noise through a one-pole lowpass + slow tremolo gusts,
  two decorrelated channels; volume envelope rises during the
  staircase/climb (shots 3–4), drops near-silence at the tower and during
  the dialogue.
- **Drone score:** three layered sine waves (55 Hz, 82.41 Hz, 110 Hz), very
  low volume, fading in after the opening, fading out during the dialogue
  exchange, returning softly at the end and fading out with the picture.
- **Chime:** two sine partials (880 Hz + 1318.51 Hz) with exponential decay,
  struck once at the device confirmation (~73.5 s).
- Mixed with a tanh soft-clip to prevent clipping, encoded AAC 192k.
(First attempt used ffmpeg filter-graph envelopes; the `clip()` expression
evaluated to NaN at filter init, so synthesis moved to numpy — same design,
deterministic and documented.)

## 7. Text on screen
- Subtitles (burned in, libass, DejaVu Sans): the chapter's dialogue only —
  "Welcome, Acolyte." / a silent beat where he is stopped from introducing
  himself / "Have you made your choice?" / "I have." /
  "I am applying to become a Keeper."
- The device screen text "Request confirmed." is drawn over the device in
  shot 8, fading in/out (diegetic UI, not a subtitle).
- End card: title + "Adapted from Snowmoon (GPL v3) by Vitalik Buterin" +
  chapter line + production credit line.

## 8. Assembly & verification
`build.sh` runs the whole pipeline end to end and prints `ffprobe`
duration/resolution for the final file. A claim still is extracted from the
lit portion of shot 7 (no subtitle on that frame), and a 3×3 contact sheet
(one frame per shot + end card) is generated for QC: shot order, subtitle
legibility, no broken frames.

## 9. Honest limitations
- No true motion footage: all movement is Ken Burns on stills. If the video
  generator becomes available, shots 3–4 are the first upgrade candidates.
- shot6 has ~12–13 seated figures, not exactly the novel's ten.
- The tower shape, exact chair positions and skyline details are invented —
  the novel describes stone walls and open sky, not the tower's silhouette.
- Audio is synthesized ambience, not recorded foley or composed music.
- Nothing has been published or submitted anywhere; the bounty claim step
  (public post + poidh claim) is intentionally left to the project owner.

## 10. Build troubleshooting log (for future reproducibility)
- Audio stage, attempt 1: unescaped commas inside `between()` in an `-af
  volume=` expression were parsed as filter separators ("No such filter").
  Fixed by escaping commas (`\,`).
- Audio stage, attempt 2: only the first `-i sine=...` had `-f lavfi`;
  the rest were treated as filenames. Fixed by adding `-f lavfi` to each.
- Audio stage, attempt 3: the envelope used `clip()`; at filter init ffmpeg
  evaluates with `t=NaN`, and `clip(NaN,…)` → NaN → "Invalid value NaN for
  volume". Replaced the whole filter-graph approach with deterministic numpy
  synthesis in `make_audio.py` (fixed seed 7).
- Final stage: an escaped apostrophe (`Keeper\'s`) inside a drawtext `text=`
  value broke ffmpeg's quote parsing and unquoted the rest of the chain
  ("No such filter"). Reworded the end-card line to "Keeper Confirmation"
  (no apostrophe).
- Device-screen text was first placed too large and off-centre (spilling
  onto the hand); re-measured the screen centre from a rendered frame and
  rebuilt with fontsize 32 at (1280, 652).
