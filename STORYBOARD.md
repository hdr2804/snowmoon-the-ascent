# THE ASCENT — Snowmoon Chapter 6 (Keeper Confirmation)
Storyboard v1 — awaiting Usam's approval before any production.

Bounty: poidh mainnet #34 "Bring Snowmoon to Life"
Source: Snowmoon by Vitalik Buterin (GPL v3), Chapter 6, Rainmoon 13, midday.
Format: 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080, ~85 seconds, 8 shots.
Dialogue: subtitled only (no spoken voice, no lip-sync — all faces are hidden
by Veridian privacy robes, which is faithful to the scene's anonymity theme).
Audio: wind, stone footsteps, robe rustle, low drone score; silence during
the dialogue exchange; a single soft chime at confirmation.

## Verified facts from the text (do not contradict)
- Midday, cold air. Gladias walks from Kalimar district; path merges into
  forest, stone-brick houses, a sky bridge, a tunnel, a Y-junction; he turns
  left (only the second time in his life).
- A straight outdoor staircase, 200 metres tall, on a mountainside. He climbs
  ~10 minutes in his privacy robe, warm despite the cold.
- Top: tunnel, two more floors of stairs, then an open top floor of a tower:
  stone walls, open sky above.
- Ten Order members sit in chairs around the edge, all in privacy robes.
- Beside Gladias: a green circle, larger and brighter than usual. He taps his
  watch to it; previously unnoticed wall lights ignite in a unique green shade.
- The exchange is solemn and mostly silent; the members must not know his
  name — cryptography verifies the right profile. He offers his nullifier
  digits as his name; some members tense, others lean in.
- He confirms his choice by tapping his hand device to the circle a second
  time. The device screen reads: "Request confirmed." Chapter ends there.
- Gladias: age and physical appearance are NOT stated in the novel. He wears
  a hooded Veridian privacy robe with a removable face covering, a neck band,
  and heeled shoes (he is shorter than average). His face is never shown in
  this film — by design.

## Shots
1. 0:00-0:10 — THE PATH. Wide. A lone robed figure walks away from camera
   where a city street dissolves into forest; large stone-brick houses.
   Slow push-in. Wind + distant city hum fading into birds/wind.
2. 0:10-0:18 — THE TURN. Sky bridge, then tunnel; Y-junction. The figure
   pauses, then turns left. A beat of stillness before the turn. Score enters.
3. 0:18-0:32 — THE STAIRCASE. Extreme wide: a straight 200 m staircase cut
   into the mountainside; the figure is tiny, climbing. Slow zoom. Footsteps
   on stone, breathing, robe rustle; cold midday light.
4. 0:32-0:42 — THE CLIMB. Closer side view, endless steps above; close insert
   of the watch and hand device against the robe. The climb does not cut —
   duration is the point.
5. 0:42-0:50 — THE TOP. Tunnel dark, two floors of stairs, then daylight
   opens: open-air tower top, stone walls, sky above. Figure steps into the
   centre and stops.
6. 0:50-1:00 — THE CIRCLE. Wide circle shot: ten robed figures seated around
   the edge, motionless. Beside Gladias, a green circle pulses — larger and
   brighter than it should be. Near-silence.
7. 1:00-1:15 — THE CONFIRMATION (PART 1). Close: watch taps the circle. Wall
   lights ignite one by one in a unique green. Subtitled exchange:
   "Welcome, Acolyte." / (he begins to introduce himself; is stopped — they
   must not know his name) / "Have you made your choice?" / "I have." /
   "I am applying to become a Keeper." Several seconds of silence; some
   figures tense, a few lean forward.
8. 1:15-1:25 — REQUEST CONFIRMED. Close: hand device taps the circle a second
   time. Screen: "Request confirmed." Hold the wide circle in green light,
   then cut to black. End card: title, "Adapted from Snowmoon (GPL v3) by
   Vitalik Buterin", production credit line.
